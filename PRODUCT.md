# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no framework, no build step (user's choice). Hostable on any static host (GitHub Pages, Netlify, Vercel). The product catalogue lives in a data structure so it can become a store later.

## Users

- People with disabilities who need a device adapted to their body and routine (grip, feeding, writing, hygiene, mobility aids, etc.).
- Family members and caregivers who search and order on their behalf, often on a phone, often worried and short on time.
- Occupational therapists and clinics looking for devices for their patients, for consulting, for training (Trilha da Inovação), or to resell as partners.

Many visitors have low vision, motor impairments, or cognitive fatigue, and use zoom, screen readers, or enlarged text.

## Product Purpose

Ocupacional 3D designs and 3D-prints personalized assistive technology devices. An occupational therapist designs them. Based in Recife-PE, ships across Brazil. The site has to explain what they make and how the process works, then turn a visit into a conversation (WhatsApp or form) for a quote.

Success: a visitor understands within seconds that this is assistive tech made to measure by a therapist, and starts a WhatsApp conversation or sends the form.

## Positioning

Clinical reasoning plus fabrication in the same small team: an occupational therapist (Roberta Falcão) assesses and indicates; a pharmacist/innovation researcher (Clarissa Braga) models and prints. Each device is made to measure, not picked from a generic catalogue, with follow-up after delivery.

## Operating Context

- Flow: assessment → indication → 3D production → delivery in 3 to 7 business days → follow-up.
- Services: personalized devices, Kits O3D, consulting (assessment, indication, post-delivery follow-up), courses and training (Trilha da Inovação), resale in partner clinics.
- Orders and quotes go through WhatsApp (81) 98459-2258; the contact form validates and opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message. E-mail: ocupacional3d@gmail.com. Instagram: @ocupacional3d.
- The online store is being shut down. No e-commerce now; the products section must be ready to become a store later.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- No backend. Form → WhatsApp deep link (wa.me/5581984592258).
- Text size control (4+ levels), high-contrast toggle, persisted in localStorage.
- Must meet WCAG 2.2 AA, 200% zoom with no horizontal scroll, works with NVDA/VoiceOver.
- Undecided: course links, prices, product catalogue contents, video tutorials.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Ocupacional 3D (O3D). Slogan: "Tecnologia que se adapta às suas necessidades".
- Colors (binding): lime green #B0F73D, navy #000065, white #FFFFFF, black #000000. Never white text on lime; on lime, use navy.
- Type (binding): Montserrat for headings; Atkinson Hyperlegible for body text.
- Voice: welcoming, human, close; technical without being cold. Short sentences, plain language. Brazilian Portuguese.
- Women-founded business (Roberta Falcão and Clarissa Braga, both UFPE 2018).

## Evidence on Hand

- Founder bios (from the brief): Roberta Falcão — occupational therapist, UFPE 2018, specialist in adult neurological rehabilitation, lecturer. Clarissa Braga — pharmacist, UFPE 2018, MSc in therapeutic innovation (UFPE 2024), in charge of 3D printing and modelling.
- No product photos, founder photos, testimonials, client logos, course links, video tutorials, or SVG logo yet. All marked placeholders; never fabricate testimonials, clients, or numbers.

## Product Principles

1. Legibility first: if a reader with low vision can't read it, it isn't done.
2. A person, not a store: every path leads to a conversation with the therapist.
3. Show the process: assessment and follow-up are the difference; make them visible.
4. Honest content: real testimonials only; placeholders are labelled as placeholders.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA minimum on every color pair; text-size control on every page; high contrast mode; respects prefers-reduced-motion and prefers-color-scheme; 44×44 px touch targets; keyboard-complete; skip link; one h1; landmarks; lang="pt-BR"; captions and transcripts on videos.
