## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is built the way the devices are: layer by layer, to measure. It refuses the soft-blue clinic template with stock smiles and rounded white cards.

OWN-WORLD: White build plate, navy (#000065) as filament: full-width navy strata carrying fine horizontal layer lines. Lime (#B0F73D) is only "the layer printing now": highlight bands under words, primary buttons, active ruler marks, always with navy text. Montserrat 800 headlines set in stacked lines; Atkinson Hyperlegible body. Square-ish corners (2px), thick 3px rules, numbered layer labels ("camada 01") in Montserrat caps.

STORY: A visitor sees this is assistive tech made to measure by an occupational therapist, follows the five layers of the process, meets the two founders, and opens WhatsApp.
